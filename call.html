Call Us — AI Assistant

Call Kent Pizzeria and our AI assistant takes your pizza order — short answers, one question at a time, and it confirms every item before finishing. It never asks for card details.

Demo only. The AI calling agent is not connected yet. This page shows how it will work once the Retell agent is set up. No real phone number is published for this fictional restaurant.

🤖 Talk to the AI assistant

Order by phone: tell the assistant what you want, confirm your delivery address, and it places the order. You pay afterwards by secure payment link.

The Retell AI agent for Kent Pizzeria has not been created yet. Once it is set up, this button will open the agent so you can call and order by voice. Demo only — no call is placed from this page today.

How a call works

  1. You call and the AI assistant greets you.
  2. You say which pizzas you want — it checks the menu and prices.
  3. It confirms your order and delivery address.
  4. You get a secure payment link by text or email — money goes to the restaurant, never through the assistant.
  5. Your pizza is cooked and a courier delivers it. Track it on the Track Order page.
