Call Us — AI Assistant

Call Kent Pizzeria and our AI assistant takes your pizza order — short answers, one question at a time, and it confirms every item before finishing. It never asks for card details.

Demo only. Kent Pizzeria is fictional. The AI assistant takes practice orders only: nothing is cooked, charged, or delivered, and it never asks for card details. Calls use free demo minutes, so the line may be unavailable once they run out.

How a call works

  1. You call and the AI assistant greets you.
  2. You say which pizzas you want — it checks the menu and prices.
  3. It confirms your order and delivery address.
  4. You get a secure payment link by text or email — money goes to the restaurant, never through the assistant.
  5. Your pizza is cooked and a courier delivers it. Track it on the Track Order page.
